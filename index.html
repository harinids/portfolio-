§ 02 — Primary Findings

Research Papers

IEEE Transactions on Computational Social Systems · 2026
DAMU: Drift-Aware Machine Unlearning for Adaptive Hate Speech Detection Under Temporal Distribution Shift

Problem: Hate-speech classifiers degrade as language and platform norms drift over time; full retraining is required to unlearn stale patterns, making continuous compliance prohibitively expensive.

Method: A four-phase pipeline coupling ADWIN drift signals directly to SISA shard deactivation — automating forget-set construction and removing the need for manual specification. Drift detection → forget-set construction → SISA unlearning → ensemble aggregation.

Drift TPR 1.00 / FPR 0.00 Forget-set accuracy −13.4 pp Retain-set accuracy +6.5 pp Forget-set loss +0.2207 Retain-set loss −0.0953 38,000-sample dataset (Davidson + OLID)
In Preparation
Manuscript in preparation · 2026
FDAM-UL: Federated Drift-Aware Machine Unlearning

Problem: Satisfying GDPR Art. 17 ("right to erasure") in federated settings is difficult without exposing client data — central unlearning approaches break the federated privacy guarantee.

Method: A fully decentralized federated framework integrating ADWIN + KS-test drift detection with EWC-regularized gradient ascent and influence-function-based unlearning. No raw client data leaves the device.

ε-forgetting bound ε ≤ 0.05 (closed-form) MIA resistance ≤ 0.55 GDPR Art. 17 compliance target
In Preparation
Manuscript in preparation · 2026
Continual Unlearning with Memory Consolidation in Large Language Models

Problem: LLMs need to selectively forget harmful or private knowledge without catastrophic forgetting of essential capabilities — and without full retraining.

Method: EWC-based selective unlearning + memory consolidation on GPT-2 with LoRA; evaluated on TOFU and HateXplain benchmarks across retain/forget/eval splits. Built two novel components: Knowledge Auditor and Stability–Plasticity Controller, balancing new learning against knowledge retention.
